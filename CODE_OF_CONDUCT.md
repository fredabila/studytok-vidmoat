# Code of Conduct

StudyGraph is built for students, by people who want to help them learn. Everyone taking part
in this project (issues, pull requests, discussions) is expected to:

* Be respectful and patient. Assume good intent; disagree about ideas, not people.
* Welcome newcomers and first-time contributors. Explain, don't belittle.
* Keep feedback specific and constructive. "This breaks the quiz hold on iOS because…" beats
  "this is wrong".
* Respect students' privacy. Never add data collection that identifies a person, and never
  share event logs or user data outside the project.
* Credit others' work and stay within the licences of stock media and third-party services.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and sustained disruption are not tolerated.
Maintainers may edit, remove or lock contributions that break these rules and may block
repeat offenders.

To report a problem, open an issue marked *conduct* or email the maintainers (see the
repository's About section). Reports are handled privately.
