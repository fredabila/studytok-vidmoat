# StudyTok — Help & FAQ

Plain answers for students, parents and the curious. For the technical side see
[ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md); for how we use data see [Privacy](#privacy) below.

## The basics

**What is StudyTok?**
A feed of short, narrated study videos made for *your* syllabus. You swipe like on TikTok, but
every video teaches one thing from your course, in one of 32 formats (explainers, myth-busts,
exam drills, flashcards, stories, chats, tier lists…), and most end with a quick check.

**Where do the videos come from?**
They are written and produced while you scroll: an AI writer drafts the lesson for your unit and
level, a narrator reads it, real diagrams and stock footage are placed, and the result plays live
in Vidmoat's player. Nothing is pre-recorded; videos already made are kept in a shared library so
the next student on that unit gets them instantly.

**Why do I need a Vidmoat account?**
StudyTok runs on Vidmoat's video technology and Vidmoat is the identity it trusts. Signing in
asks only to read your name and email, keeps your course and progress across devices, and later
lets you make lessons on your own Vidmoat credits if you want to.

**It says "Vidmoat accounts are for adults". I'm 15.**
Vidmoat accounts are 18+. A parent or guardian signs in with *their* Vidmoat account, chooses
"My child", enters your age and ticks the consent box. StudyTok then pitches lessons at your
level and age, and under-13s get gentler lessons without opinion-led formats.

## Using the feed

**How does it decide what to show me?**
Two steps. First it picks a *unit*: your syllabus in order, what you have engaged with, what is
due for review (spaced repetition: a unit comes back 1, 3, 7 and 21 days after a correct answer,
and 20 minutes after a wrong one), minus what you just saw. Then it picks the *video* for that
unit: how well others watched it, which formats you tend to watch through, variety, how long you
have been scrolling, how you did on the last quizzes, and context such as whether you are
walking. Tap **Why** on any video to see the reasons. The full write-up is
[ALGORITHM.md](ALGORITHM.md).

**The video stopped at the question. Is it broken?**
No, that is on purpose. The video holds at the quick check until you tap an answer (or Skip), so
the answer is never read out before you have tried.

**What is a "format"?**
The shape of the video: an explainer with diagrams, a myth-vs-fact with stamps, a five-things
countdown, an exam drill with worked steps, a spot-the-mistake, a POV ("you are a red blood
cell"), a cheat sheet that fills in, a ten-second challenge… Each has its own look, pace, music
and narrator. The badge under each video names it. All 32 are in [FORMATS.md](FORMATS.md).

**I got a random fun video that was not on my course. Why?**
That was a *breather*. If you leave a couple of lessons early, get a few quiz answers wrong in a
row, or have been scrolling a long while, the feed drops in one short, fun video from next door
to your subject (animal superpowers for biology, famous software bugs for computer science),
then goes back to your units. It never counts towards your progress. Tap **Why** on it to see the
reason, and tap **Less** if you would rather never see them.

**Can I pick my own topics?**
Yes. Tune (sliders icon on a video) → *Your own topics*: type anything ("Chess openings",
"The Ashanti Empire") and lessons follow. In the Library you can also untick units you have
mastered; the feed follows what stays ticked.

**How do I refresh?**
Pull down on the first video, or on the Library or Profile; or tap the refresh icon in the
header. Coming back after a long break refreshes on its own.

**Why does it say "while you wait"?**
Your own lesson is being made (about a minute). Meanwhile you get a ready lesson from the library
for your level, and yours appears when it is done.

## Progress, streaks and sharing

**What is my member number?**
Your place in the order students joined: #1 joined first. It is on your Profile and on every card
you share.

**What are the stat tiles and badges?**
Day streak (days in a row with at least one lesson), lessons watched, quick-check score and units
mastered. Badges appear for a 3-day streak, 80%+ on quizzes, mastered units and 25+ lessons.

**Why do old units come back as flashcards and quizzes instead of lessons?**
That is review. When spaced repetition brings a unit back, StudyTok serves retrieval practice
(flashcards, true or false, a ten-second challenge, an exam drill) rather than explaining it
again, because recalling an answer yourself is what makes it stick. Tap **Why** on the video to
see "due for review".

**What is the ring on my Profile?**
Your daily goal: three lessons a day. It fills as you watch and turns green when done. Noti
will also tell you when your streak is about to lapse and when units are due.

**What does "unit mastered" mean?**
Enough lessons completed and quick checks answered correctly on that unit (mastery ≥ 70%). You
get a burst with Noti, and the unit comes back later only for review.

**How do I share?**
*Share recap* (Profile and Library, every week), *Share* on a level-up, and *Share my card* on
your Profile make an image card you can post anywhere. On a phone it opens the share sheet; on a
computer the image downloads and the caption is copied.

**I got a quick check wrong. What happens?**
It comes back. The question returns as a *recall card* in your feed after a while (usually your
next session), with the explanation if you miss it again and a button to rewatch the lesson. Get
it right twice, a couple of days apart, and it is retired. Your Library shows how many are
waiting under "questions to fix".

**Can StudyTok plan around my exam date?**
Yes. Add the date when you set up (or edit) your studies. From 45 days out your feed leans
towards revision of the units you have started, more lessons become retrieval practice
(flashcards, true or false, drills), and a countdown appears on your Library and Profile.

**How do I find something in my Library?**
The search box under the Library tabs filters whatever tab you are on: units and packs on
Course, lessons on Saved and History. The pills under it say how many matches each tab has;
tap one to switch. If nothing matches, one tap adds what you typed as a topic. On a computer,
press `/` to jump to the box and the arrow keys to move between tabs. Long lists scroll inside
their own box, so the page never becomes endless.

**Can I start over?**
Yes, from your Profile under *Start fresh*. *Reset my feed* clears your progress, streak,
history, missed questions and everything the algorithm learned about your tastes; your course,
saved lessons, settings and member number stay, and new lessons start from the beginning of
your syllabus. *Reset everything* also clears your course, saved lessons and settings, and you
set up again. Your account, member number, invites and free-lesson count always stay.

**What is recall mode (the "key terms hidden" chip)?**
The video plays with its key terms blanked out of the on-screen text and the narration muted, so
you have to supply them yourself: that is retrieval practice, the thing that makes knowledge
stick. Tap the chip to reveal the terms and hear the narration. It is on for lessons that come
back for review; in *Tune › Playback* you can turn it off or have it on for every lesson. Captions,
music and narration audio can be switched there too, and take effect on the video you are
watching.

**Can I jump around inside a lesson?**
Yes. The marks on the progress bar are the lesson's scenes; tap one to jump there.

**What is the Explore tab?**
Community packs: courses people have shared (the ideas of a book, notes from a class, what a
project does). Search them, filter by topic, open "What you will learn", and *Add to Library*
puts the pack's units in your course. The Library only shows what you have added.

**Can I bring my own material, or promote what I am doing?**
Yes. StudyTok is open source: add a *pack*, one Markdown file in the repo with the ideas of a
book, your course notes or what your project does, written in your own words, plus optional
labelled images or clips. Once merged, anyone can add it from the Explore tab or your
link `studytok.vidmoat.com/?pack=<slug>`, lessons are made from it, and every lesson shows your
name, link and call to action. The format and the rules are in
[content/README.md](https://github.com/fredabila/studytok-vidmoat/blob/main/content/README.md).

**What are the cards from Noti between videos?**
*Moments*: a tip about StudyTok, a piece of news or an offer, shown at most every six lessons and
three times a day. Tap anywhere on one (or swipe) to carry on. Each appears once, then not again
for weeks.

**Can I share a video with someone who doesn't have StudyTok?**
Yes. *Share lesson* on any video copies a public link (`studytok.vidmoat.com/v/…`) that opens
without an account: the lesson plays in the browser, with the quick check, and the page says it
was shared by your member number (never your name). Whoever taps "Get lessons for your own
syllabus" from it counts as your invite.

**Who is Noti?**
The sticky-note study buddy on your Profile and Library. Noti cheers on streaks, thinks when
reviews are due, and sleeps when a list is empty.

## Credits

**Is it free?**
Your first lessons (15 by default) are made on StudyTok's Vidmoat credits. Watching anything
already in the library is always free.

**What happens after the free lessons?**
Connect your own Vidmoat account (Profile → Credits → *Use my Vidmoat account*). New lessons you
trigger are then made with your credits, about 20 each. If you run out, the app points you to
credit packs or a plan upgrade on Vidmoat, and everything already made keeps playing.

**Does StudyTok spend my credits without asking?**
No. Only lessons you trigger spend credits, only after you connect your account, and it never
renders, exports, or touches your Speech Lab voices or Cast. You can disconnect any time.

## Installing

**Can I install it?**
Yes. On Android/Chrome and desktop, tap *Install the app* on the landing page or Profile. On
iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share, then *Add to Home Screen*. It opens full screen and keeps
your sign-in.

**Can StudyTok remind me?**
Yes, if you want it to. Install the app, open your Profile and tap *Enable reminders*. You get at
most one notification a day, and only when it matters: your streak is about to end and you have
not studied yet today (sent in the early evening, your time), or two or more units are due for a
review. On iPhone this needs iOS 16.4 or later and the app added to your Home Screen; in a
browser tab Safari cannot deliver notifications. Turn it off again from the same card, or in your
phone's notification settings.

**Videos are stuttering.**
Close other heavy apps and make sure you are on a recent browser. Only one video plays at a
time, footage is 720p and projects run at 24 fps precisely to keep phones smooth; if it still
struggles, tell us the phone model.

## Privacy

**What do you collect?**
How you use the feed: which videos you watch and for how long, where you leave them, pauses,
loops, quick-check answers, likes, saves, "less", what you change in settings, and coarse device
context (screen size, connection type, time zone, orientation, battery level bucket). This is what
makes the feed yours. The full list is in [EVENTS.md](EVENTS.md).

**Do you use my phone's sensors?**
If your phone allows it (iPhones ask; Android allows motion sensors for secure sites), the app
reads the motion sensors *on the device* and reduces them to a single word: held, lying flat, or
moving. Only that word is sent, never the sensor readings. It is used to pick lighter,
narration-led lessons when you are walking and calmer ones when you are lying down late at
night, and to pause the video if the phone is dropped. You can turn the browser's motion-sensor
permission off for the site at any time; the app works the same without it.

**Do you use the camera or microphone?**
No. There is no face or gaze tracking and no audio recording.

**Is any of this sold or shared?**
No. Events stay on StudyTok's own server (Upstash Redis), tied to your StudyTok user id, and are
used only to rank videos and improve the lessons. Public statistics at `/api/stats` are
aggregated across everyone with no personal data.

**What do you store from my Vidmoat account?**
Your Vidmoat account id, name and email at sign-in. If you connect your account for credits, an
encrypted access token that is used only to make videos on your account, and deleted when you
disconnect.

**Can I delete my data?**
Yes. Email the address in the repository's About section with your member number and we will
remove your profile, progress and events. (A self-service button is on the roadmap.)
